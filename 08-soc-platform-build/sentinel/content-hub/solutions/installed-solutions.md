# Installed Solutions

This document inventories the Microsoft and community solutions installed through Microsoft Sentinel Content Hub during this implementation. These solutions extend Microsoft Sentinel with analytics rules, workbooks, hunting queries, automation, threat intelligence, investigations, and operational guidance.
